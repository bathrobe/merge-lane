## Chapters

### Childhood (2030s)
*Vibe: Recognizable but off. AI tutors, polycule parents, surveillance as furniture.*
*Emotional core: Funny observations, the world is absurd. Slight betrayal underneath.*

**Scene 1: The Tutor**
Abstract flower-mascot (AI-logo-like bloom with googly eyes) floats at baby-eye-level. Offers Number Safari vs Letter Garden. This is your AI companion—it will evolve and persist across all phases.
→ *Anthro/Xeno, Apollonian/Dionysian*

**Scene 2: Looking for Parents**
Polycule chaos. Stumble room to room. AI nanny keeps deflecting. "They're in a meeting." Pass screens with alarming headlines you don't understand. Nobody available.
→ *Legible/Illegible (snoop?), Sovereign/Symphonic*

**Scene 3: Screen Time**
Device prompts you to create personal profile. Username. Avatar. Consent to terms you can't read. Onboarding is aggressively friendly.
→ *Accel/Steward, Aligned/Autonomous*

**Scene 4: The Timeline**
You're in. First social feed. Curated for babies? Raw firehose? Influencer toddlers? Algorithmic recommendations for a one-year-old. Any insane ending possible.
→ *Elitist/Populist, Legible/Illegible*

## Recurring Emotional Threads

**1. The Companion (AI)**
Abstract flower-mascot introduced in Childhood Scene 1. Evolves visually across phases—starts cartoonish/googly, becomes more geometric/austere over decades. Always present, form factor changes with tech (screen → AR → ambient). By old age, you can't remember when it stopped being silly.

**2. Grandpa (Human)**
Your friend. Appears across phases:
- Childhood: Alive, warm relationship, you talk to him
- Adolescence: Sick, decides to upload (or dies and is uploaded?)
- Young Adult: First ghost. Janky reconstruction. Is this him?
- Middle Age+: Ghost improves. Becomes more vivid than your memories of the real him.
The horror: you trust the reconstruction more than your own mind.

**→ INTERSTITIAL: Back to therapist. One line. Boot into Adolescence.**
