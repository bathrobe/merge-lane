## Constraints

- ~20-25 images total (backdrops only, no baked text)
- Single main thread first, branching later
- 3-4 scenes per chapter
- CSS transitions between scenes (fade + glitch flicker)

## Exposition channels

- Scrolling text per scene
- Clickable hotspots (optional flavor text)
- In-world documents (emails, memos, manuals)
- Therapist interstitials (pure text, no image needed)

**1. The Companion (AI)**

_Reference image: `notes/companion-pinwheel.jpg`_

White circular center containing two large expressive eyes (with shine dots) and a small curved smile. Flat vector style, clean edges, no outlines. ### Young Adult (2050s)

_Vibe: Post-scarcity tensions. Superintelligence is here. Work optional. Death a class issue._
_Emotional core: Purpose, drift, who you're becoming vs who you thought you'd be. The sad numbness of your 20s — adulthood arrived but meaning didn't._

**⚠️ ART DIRECTION — MANDATORY FOR ALL SCHOLASTIC-BG GENERATIONS THIS PHASE:**

This phase looks DIFFERENT from previous ones. We're decades into the future, barreling toward singularity. The world has changed.

**The Apple Product Aesthetic:**
- White plastic curvy blobs, seamless — no visible seams, screws, or joints
- Soft gradients everywhere, the trademark "AI gradient glow" on edges
- Color palette: predominantly white with gradient accents (think space gray to white fades, soft coral/teal glows)
- Materials: glass, brushed aluminum, smooth polymers. ABSENT: raw wood, visible fabric textures, industrial grit

**Tech Visibility:** Less is more. Technology is so integrated you barely see it. No floating AR chaos — it's ambient, invisible, assumed. The Expanse's Earth, HER's Los Angeles. Spacey sci-fi but grounded.

**Context Matters:** Thanksgiving is still a house, not an Apple Store. Apply the aesthetic with subtlety — a home in 2050 has this design language baked in, but it's still a home. Warmth filtered through minimalism.

**Emotional Tone:** Numb. Clean. A little sad. Everything is optimized and something is missing.

**References:** HER (Spike Jonze), The Expanse (Earth scenes), Apple product photography, soft sci-fi interiors.

**Image Generation Learnings (from Thanksgiving scene):**

- Specify AI gradient glow colors explicitly: "soft multicolor AI gradient glows on edges (peach, teal, soft purple)"
- FPV from seated position = plate/utensils in foreground
- Attach character refs when they appear (grandpa, cousin)

### Middle Age (2060s-70s)

_Vibe: ASI achieved. Companion is President of Earth. Metaverse is default. Mars colony exists._
_Emotional core: Acceptance, comedy, the horror that isn't horrifying. You adapted._

**⚠️ ART DIRECTION — MANDATORY FOR ALL SCHOLASTIC-BG GENERATIONS THIS PHASE:**

This phase looks DIFFERENT from Young Adult. We've gone from Apple minimalism to something more totalizing, more seductive, more obviously "simulation."

**The Chrome + Warm Light + Pearlescent Aesthetic:**
- Chrome and reflective surfaces everywhere — cold metal made inviting
- Warm ambient lighting: amber, sunset glow, soft gold. The simulation wants you comfortable
- Pearlescent/holographic sheen on surfaces — that rainbow oil-slick shimmer, dreamy, too beautiful
- Everything feels like a luxury spa crossed with a spaceship

**The Uncanny Comfort:**
- Color depth is too perfect, reflections too clean
- Reality as premium content — rendered, optimized, frictionless
- Cold materials (chrome, glass, metal) but warm feeling
- You know it's artificial. It's still nice.

**What's Different from Young Adult:**
- YA was white plastic, seamless curves, gradient glows — Apple Store Earth
- Middle Age is chrome mirrors, warm light, pearlescent shimmer — the simulation stopped pretending to be reality and became something better

**Emotional Tone:** Seductive. Cozy. Uncanny. The horror that isn't horrifying.

**References:** Blade Runner 2049 interiors, high-end render porn, spa architecture, chrome Jeff Koons, holographic packaging.

**⚠️ STYLE-LOCK PRIORITY:**
The 90s pre-rendered CD-ROM aesthetic (from `scholastic-bg/references/style-lock.md`) ALWAYS takes priority. Use the prefix/suffix verbatim. The chrome/warm light/pearlescent direction is a MODIFIER to the base style, not a replacement. Keep:

## Therapist Frame

**Visual reference:** John Lilly's "Programming the Human Biocomputer" cover. Clinical but consciousness-adjacent. 70s research-lab-meets-enlightenment.

**Opening:**

- Meet the therapist. Warm but bureaucratic.
- Minimal explanation. Don't reveal what's coming.
- "Let's begin. For the file."

**Interstitials (between phases):**

- Black screen. White text. Therapist's voice.
- Brief — one or two lines, reactive to what just happened.
- "Interesting. Let's continue."
- "That choice will be noted."
- No image. Keeps it cheap, keeps it mysterious.

**Closing (post-merge decision):**

- Return to therapist. See him again.
- Acknowledges what you went through.
- Presents results: 8 axes, visualized in same aesthetic as office (cream/beige, dossier feel, typewriter fonts, institutional warmth).
- Delivers seed phrase: "This is your identifier. Keep it safe. You may need it again."
- Hints at more: "Based on your responses, you may be suited for... further involvement. The Reading Room will review your file."
- Door opens, doesn't over-explain. Mystery preserved.

**Tone throughout:** Never judges your choices. All axes are valid. The society needs all types.
