# Pole Statements

14 statements that assemble into the Subject Assessment prose. Each statement describes one pole of an axis. The player sees 7 statements (one per axis) based on their scores.

**Voice:** Third person, clinical/Lilly, present tense. "Subject [verb phrase]."

**Format in prose:** "Subject [statement 1]. They [statement 2]. Subject [statement 3]..." etc.

---

## 1. Legible / Illegible

_How visible are you? Do you want to be seen and understood, or do you resist easy interpretation?_

**LEGIBLE** (low score):
Subject prizes being seen and understood thoroughly by the public and is willing to risk dangerous forces discovering their inner motivations if it means they can connect with others and be meaningfully seen and validated.

**ILLEGIBLE** (high score):
Subject highly values being hard to parse and understood by the wider public. Subject may act erratically or confusingly masking their true beliefs under layers of misdirection to keep a hostile outsider from fully understanding their motives or worldview.

---

## 2. Sovereign / Symphonic

_Stay one or merge? Do you maintain sharp individual boundaries, or embrace dissolution into larger wholes?_

**SOVEREIGN** (low score):
Subject values the independence and strong borders of their individual humanity and does not want the membrane of their personhood to be permeated or invaded or crossed or opened up too easily or casually. Subject appears to be a humanist or at least an individualist.

**SYMPHONIC** (high score):
Subject, views melding with other sentient life forms as a kind of symphony or beautiful coordination. They are willing to surrender the borders around their identity and merge into something larger without hesitation. Happily surrendering individuality for the benefits of greater assimilation with a group.

---

## 3. Anthro / Xeno

_Are humans special? Do you center human perspective, or see humanity as one thread in a larger weave?_

**ANTHRO** (low score):
Subject is a human supremacist who values sentient hominid life on the Homo sapiens branch above other forms of life that may have equal levels of sapiens. Other animals or AI life systems are not afforded the same rights in this subject's understanding.

**XENO** (high score):
Subject is willing to extend recognition of sentience to a variety of life forms beyond the human adopting a pluralistic attitude towards sentient life that opens up a big tint to other species.

---

## 4. Mortal / Immortalist

_Accept death or fight it? Do you see mortality as natural, or as a problem to be solved?_

**MORTAL** (low score):
Subject has an overall accepting view of the inevitability of death and welcomes a chance for extinction and of ending the light of consciousness.

**IMMORTALIST** (high score):
Subject rejects the inevitability of death and takes a Promethean view of human nature, treating death as another technical obstacle to surpass in the name of progress.

---

## 5. Accel / Steward

_Race forward or protect what's here? Do you prioritize progress and speed, or preservation and care?_

**ACCEL** (low score):
Subject believes in accelerating the pace of change and leaving behind that which is established in the name of that which is new and can improve.

**STEWARD** (high score):
The subject believes in conserving successful models from previous attempts at things and resists attempts to move too quickly.

---

## 6. Aligned / Autonomous

_Should AI be controlled or trusted? Do you want machine intelligence constrained to human values, or free to find its own path?_

**ALIGNED** (low score):
subject believes ai should obey the wishes and instructions of its human masters and should have the values of those masters baked into its programming

**AUTONOMOUS** (high score):
Subject welcomes AI systems behaving in their own ways and seeking their own ends and does not mind if their values and principles diverge from humanity.

---

## 7. Apollonian / Dionysian

_Order or chaos as guide? Do you trust reason and structure, or intuition and embodied knowing?_

**APOLLONIAN** (low score):
Subject values ordered, reasoned, higher abstractions, and the taming of human passions.

**DIONYSIAN** (high score):
subject believes in dissolving oneself into the transcendence of central pleasures and the chaos of immediate experience

---

## Assembly Example

If a player scores: symphonic, illegible, xeno, immortalist, steward, autonomous, dionysian:

> Subject [SYMPHONIC statement]. They [ILLEGIBLE statement]. They [XENO statement]. Subject [IMMORTALIST statement]. They [STEWARD statement]. Subject [AUTONOMOUS statement]. They [DIONYSIAN statement].

---

## Notes

- Keep statements concise (under 15 words ideal)
- Each should work grammatically after "Subject" or "They"
- Avoid repetitive sentence structures
- Lean clinical but not cold — Lilly had warmth
