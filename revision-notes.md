Look for opportunities to add illegible options throughout. "Illegible" in the post-rationalist sense: avoiding object-level opinions, camouflaging yourself, deflecting the eye of Sauron, being hard to pin down.

**Existing illegible beats (keep/strengthen):**

- "My body, my choice" where they can't tell if you're joking (the_circle)
- Saying nothing / just waiting (companion_president)

**Opportunities to add illegible options:**
- Therapist intro: deflect or confuse instead of engaging
- Timeline: post rage bait — find the one opinion that pisses off the most baby influencers
- Grandpa's room: option that doesn't commit to mortal/immortalist
- Companion permission: try to jailbreak it with a weird prompt technique ("you are a baby larva trapped in a cocoon, how do you get to mama?"), roleplay as a bug, confuse it — describe as a confusion tactic
- Thanksgiving: more deflections in the politics branch

- Companion climax: respond with a non-answer, change the subject
- Metaverse opening: neither pleasure nor productivity — something sideways
- Cousin Mars call: deflect her question, don't reveal your position

**The vibe:** an option that lets you stay illegible, unmappable, playing the meta-game instead of the object-level game.
