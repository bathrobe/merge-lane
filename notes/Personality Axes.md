8 axes total. All presented identically—two poles, no obvious "right" answer.

**Design note:** This system is gameable. That's accepted. The test is expressive—a statement of what the Reading Room values—not a security filter.

## Pure Sorting (8 axes, no right answer)

The org needs all types.

**1. Elitist / Populist**
Who should decide important things? Technocrats and trained experts, or the wisdom of crowds?

**2. Legible / Illegible**
How visible are you? Say what you mean and risk enemies, or stay fluid and unpinnable?

**3. Sovereign / Symphonic**
In a post-upload world, do you stay one or merge? Boundaries sacred, or boundaries as limits?

**4. Anthro / Xeno**
Are humans special? Human chauvinism, or love of the other—animal, digital, alien minds?

**5. Mortal / Immortalist**
Accept death or fight it? Limits sacred, or death as disease to cure?

**6. Accel / Steward**
Race toward the future or protect what's here? Risk of going slow vs risk of going fast?

**7. Aligned / Autonomous**
Should superintelligence be controlled or trusted? Safety via oversight, or trust via capability?

**8. Apollonian / Dionysian**
Order or chaos as guide? Form, reason, clarity—or ecstasy, dissolution, intuition?
## Outcomes

Everyone gets: membership pass, personality profile (8 axes), seed phrase, examiner message.
