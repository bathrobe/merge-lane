## Environment Palette

**Primary:**
- Teal / aqua blue
- Deep purple / indigo
- Warm amber / terracotta / copper

**Accent:**
- Forest green
- Gold

**NOT stark white** — warmer, darker, theatrical

## Textures & Materials

- Marble and stone (checkered floors, pedestals)
- Warm wood (display cases, paneling)
- Copper and brass fixtures
- Leaf/organic sculptural shapes
- Early CGI sheen on rendered surfaces

## Composition Patterns

- Floating/suspended objects (specimens, artifacts)
- Theatrical framing (curtains, arches, doorways as portals)
- Museum exhibit display language
- Objects-as-specimens on pedestals
- Track lighting / spotlights in darkness

## Mood

- Museum-at-night energy
- Knowledge as expedition/adventure
- Inviting darkness (not sterile brightness)
- Slightly mysterious, wonder-inducing
- Children's museum / Nature Company vibe
- "Museum for no reason" — space feels educational but logic is dreamlike

## Reference Touchpoints
